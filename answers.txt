Answer 3:

Sprint Planning Meeting

       a) Discuss and determine what to complete in the next sprint

       b) Move stories from backlog to current sprint

       c) Discuss who will be responsible for what task/story

Daily Scrum/Standup Meeting 

       a) Short meeting that happens every day of the sprint

       b) Team members report their progress

       c) Discuss work plan for the day and if there are any blockers

Sprint Retrospective Meeting

       a) Occurs at the end of the sprint

       b) Discuss work completed and move incomplete work to backlog

       c) Discuss what the team can improve or do differently in the next sprint

Epic: Captures a large body of work that can be broken down into several smaller stories

Story: A software system requirement from the perspective of the user that is expressed in a few short sentences

Bug: Bugs are problems that need to be fixed that impede the progress or functionality of work

Task: Represents a task that needs to be done.
